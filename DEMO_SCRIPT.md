# 🎙️ VyaparPulse — 90-Second Hackathon Demo Script
**Track 2: FinTech & Local Commerce (HACK IT BROS '26)**

---

### [00:00 – 00:15] The Hook: The Diary Problem
> *"Meet Ramesh ji. He runs a neighborhood supermarket with over 150 credit customers. Every evening, he flips through pages of paper diaries wondering: 'Kaun kab paise dega, aur agle hafte dukan me cash ki kami toh nahi hogi?'*
> *Introducing **VyaparPulse** — the smart, voice-first ledger that turns messy Hinglish entries into clear cashflow predictions in 2 taps."*

---

### [00:15 – 00:40] Voice / Text Quick Entry (Live Demo)
> *(Open [http://localhost:5173](http://localhost:5173) on mobile view or desktop)*
> 
> 1. *"Look at Ramesh ji's Home screen. Right at the top, our AI insight engine calculates his numbers directly from SQL: **₹1,87,200 expected this week**, and alerts him: **'Call Verma Ji first — ₹24,000 overdue.'**"*
> 2. *(Tap the Quick Entry Mic button)*
> 3. *"Now a customer walks in. Ramesh ji taps the mic or types:*
>    **'Sharma ji ne ₹2,400 ka saaman 7 din udhaar pe liya.'** *(Click preset chip)*
> 4. *"In under 10 milliseconds, our deterministic Hinglish parser extracts the customer, fuzzy-matches Ramesh Sharma from 40 ledger accounts, detects ₹2,400, 7 days due date, and groceries."*
> 5. *(Tap **Save Entry**)*
> 6. *"Tap Save — boom! Done in 2 taps. No tech skills needed."*

---

### [00:40 – 01:10] FIFO Ledger & 1-Click WhatsApp Reminder
> 1. *"Let's look at Sharma Ji's ledger. The ₹2,400 invoice is added to his timeline with a due date of next week."*
> 2. *"Need to collect? Ramesh ji taps **'Send Reminder'**. VyaparPulse drafts a polite WhatsApp message with store details, amount, due date, and UPI payment link. One tap opens `wa.me` directly to the customer's chat."*
> 3. *"When Sharma ji pays, tap **'Record Payment'**. Watch what happens: we enter ₹2,400. Our backend **FIFO engine** automatically allocates it against his oldest open bills first, updating the visual settlement progress bars."*

---

### [01:10 – 01:30] Cashflow Forecasting & Closing
> 1. *(Click the **Cashflow** tab)*
> 2. *"Finally, Ramesh ji’s biggest headache — cash shortage — is solved. Our 7-day and 14-day Inflow Forecast maps every rupee expected each day based on bill terms, showing exactly who is scheduled to pay."*
> 3. *(Click the **हिन्दी** toggle in the header)*
> 4. *"With a full Hindi UI toggle and customer reliability scoring, VyaparPulse bridges the gap between paper diaries and modern fintech for India's 12 million local retailers."*
> 5. *"Thank you! We're happy to take your questions."*
